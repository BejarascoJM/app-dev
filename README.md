## About the Series
**Breaking Bad** is my favorite television series. It is a crime drama that follows the story of *Walter White*, a high school chemistry teacher who turns to producing illegal drugs after being diagnosed with cancer.

### **Main Characters**
- Walter White
- Jesse Pinkman
- Skyler White
- Hank Schrader
- Saul Goodman

### Why I Like It
1. The story is interesting and unpredictable.
2. The characters develop throughout the series.
3. The acting and cinematography are excellent.
4. Each season becomes more exciting than the last.
